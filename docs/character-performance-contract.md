The approved characters are the Chinese brazier maiden and the eighteen-year-old mine-rescue lantern maiden. Both stand left of gameplay; the skeleton and his independent table occupy the right.

## Identity and story

- Brazier maiden: eighteen-year-old Chinese adult, short natural proportions, slim flat chest, jade qipao and cowboy boots. Holds the good-fortune brazier in both hands. Quietly tends a private haunting ritual. Her death story is not yet specified.
- Lantern maiden: eighteen-year-old adult. Entered a flooding mine to rescue her father; the mine collapsed and crushed her. Continues the unfinished rescue. Father's fate is unspecified. Red coat, practical clothes, hat and lantern; searching and listening rather than posing.
- Skeleton: seated three-quarter view at his own table, fixed ghostly losing cards on the felt. Never a player opponent. Watches the player's developing hand and its resolution; otherwise idles.
- Exterior condemned and rider: occupied with the eternal hanging. Ignore ordinary gambling. Only a payout of at least 1,000 displayed credits prompts their Easter egg.

## Playback and triggers

Native performances return to their common idle pose. Independent clocks, varied idle lengths, no interruption for a reaction. Coalesce developing-hand updates so stale reactions cannot accumulate. A resolved hand supersedes a pending developing-hand reaction. Replay, restoration, reduced motion and hidden tabs do not create new reactions. Foreground exceptional-result policy must be explicit and tested. Exterior threshold is absolute payout >= 100,000 integer minor units (1,000.00 displayed credits), not a wager multiple or net profit.

## Transparency and admission

Never generate white-backed sources for masking. Use dark backgrounds for footage requiring extraction. Shipped PNGs must have real alpha; shipped WebM must decode with real alpha. No painted checkerboard. Retain partial alpha in hair and smoke, clean color contamination, inspect over dark and light contrasting backgrounds and at stage scale. Verify every clip, not just its poster. Keep smoke separate when needed for reliable edges. Do not substitute opaque footage, static images or generic CSS movement and call the native animation work complete.
